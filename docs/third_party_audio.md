# Third-Party Audio Assets

## GeneralUser GS

- Author: S. Christian Collins
- Official repository: https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS
- Used file: `assets/soundfonts/GeneralUser-GS.sf2`
- Download source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS/main/GeneralUser-GS.sf2
- Download date: 2026-08-08
- Official documentation reviewed: `documentation/README.md`, `documentation/LICENSE.txt`

## Official License And Redistribution Notes

- The official `documentation/LICENSE.txt` states that the complete work may be used without restriction for music creation and says the SoundFont bank may be used in software projects and modified to suit project needs.
- The same license text also says some included samples came from other freely available SoundFonts and that the author cannot be fully certain of every sample's original source.
- The author notes that this uncertainty may be a concern for commercial software products and asks to be informed if any restricted sample is discovered.
- The official license text asks third-party sites not to hotlink the author's download files; it says to link to the website or provide a local copy instead.
